# Print-on-Demand Guide for Orbital Pioneers + Cali

**Quick Start (Do This Week):**

1. Sign up at Printful.com (free)
2. Upload your best Cali + show designs
3. Create products (start with stickers, T-shirts, hoodies)
4. Connect to Gumroad as your main store
5. Link the merch from all 11+ websites to one central merch hub
6. Use your automated merch drop workflow to keep adding new drops

Full details below.

**Goal:** Add high-quality custom merch (our brand + Cali designs) across all your sites with zero upfront inventory cost.

## Top Recommendation: Printful (Best Overall for You)

**Why Printful is the winner for your situation:**

- Completely free to start (no monthly fees)
- Excellent print quality (one of the best in POD)
- Strong branding options (your own designs, tags, packaging inserts)
- Good integrations with Gumroad, Etsy, and custom websites
- Fast production + reliable shipping
- Easy to set up "Custom Stores" or link products

**Runner-up:** Printify (often slightly cheaper base prices, similar model).

**Avoid for now if you want full brand control:**

- Redbubble (more marketplace, less brand ownership)
- Teespring/Spring (good but less flexible for custom branding)

## How to Set It Up (Step by Step)

### 1. Create a Printful Account

- Go to printful.com
- Sign up with the project owner account email (real details used only at actual Printful / POD signup time)
- Verify your account (free)

### 2. Upload Your Designs

Focus on your brand + Cali:

- Cali the Husky designs (various poses, "This Husky Survived the Void", Pilot Wings with Cali, etc.)
- Orbital Pioneers logo + characters
- Episode-specific art
- 90s retro cartoon style graphics

Printful has a design maker + mockup generator.

### 3. Create Products

Recommended starting catalog (high margin + easy):

- T-shirts (standard + premium)
- Hoodies (especially the "Pilot Wings" style)
- Stickers (very high margin, perfect for Cali)
- Posters / Art prints
- Mugs
- Phone cases

### 4. Connect to Your Sales Channels

**Best options for you:**

**A. Gumroad (Recommended primary)**

- Printful has direct integration with Gumroad via apps or manual product linking.
- You can list products on Gumroad and fulfill via Printful.
- Customers order on your Gumroad → Printful prints and ships.

**B. Etsy**

- Printful has excellent Etsy integration.
- Great for discovery of Cali-themed items.

**C. Your 11+ Custom Websites**

- Options:
  - Link to a central "Orbital Pioneers Merch Store" (can be a simple Gumroad store or a free Printful Custom Store).
  - Use Printful's API if you want full custom checkout on your sites (more advanced).
  - For fastest: Create one main merch page (you already have `orbital-merch-hub`) and link all products there. Drive traffic from every other site to this hub.

### 5. Set Up a Central Merch Hub (If You Haven't Already)

You already have `orbital-merch-hub`. Make this the single destination:

- Link it prominently from all 11+ websites.
- Feature Cali heavily (she's your best mascot for merch).
- Use the automated merch drop workflow to keep adding new designs.

## Recommended Strategy: "Custom Brand First"

Focus on **your brand** (Orbital Pioneers + Cali) rather than generic marketplace:

1. **Primary:** Sell through Gumroad + your own sites using Printful fulfillment.
2. **Secondary:** Use Etsy for discovery (especially Cali designs).

This way customers feel like they're buying official Orbital Pioneers / Cali merch, not random marketplace stuff.
