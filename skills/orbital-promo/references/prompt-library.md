# VHS Prompt Library — Orbital Pioneers Marketing

**Brand Voice Rules (Non-Negotiable):**

- 90s Saturday morning cartoon energy (think early Toonami + Nickelodeon + Fox Kids)
- Warm family chaos + genuine heart
- Bold, direct hooks
- "Remember when cartoons felt like this?" nostalgia
- Never corporate. Never cringe. Always sincere hype.

---

## YouTube Shorts / Vertical Video Hooks

**Strong Openers (first 3 seconds):**

- "Dad just hit the snack dispenser... during an asteroid chase."
- "Zara is 15 and already cooler than every adult on this ship."
- "The wings were supposed to be lucky. They were not."
- "Echo only speaks in three beeps. Somehow he's still the smartest one here."

**CTA Styles:**

- "Full episode drops Saturday. Link in bio."
- "Want the Pilot Wings hoodie? It's in the comments."
- "This is what Saturday morning was supposed to feel like."

---

## Thumbnail Prompt Templates (Gemini / Image Models)

**Base Style:**
"1990s Saturday morning cartoon style, bold cel-shaded colors, dramatic lighting, family adventure energy, thick black outlines, vibrant palette like early 90s Nicktoons"

**High-Converting Variations:**

1. **Chaos Hook**
   "Zara calmly flying the ship while the entire cockpit explodes behind her, Alex panicking in the background, big bold yellow text 'DAD HIT THE WRONG BUTTON', 90s cartoon style"

2. **Emotional Hook**
   "Close-up of young girl (Zara) gently placing silver pilot wings into her dad's hand, soft emotional lighting, title treatment 'The Wings That Saved Us', warm nostalgic 90s cartoon aesthetic"

3. **Action Hook**
   "The Wanderer rocket ship diving between two massive asteroids, family visible in the cockpit windows, dramatic orange and teal color palette, bold title 'FIRST LEAP'"

## Ad Script Templates (15-30s)

**Format:**
[0-3s Hook] → [Problem/Chaos] → [Zara moment] → [Family heart] → [CTA]

**Example (Chaotic Energy):**

```
0-3s:  [Asteroid whooshes past camera]
VO: "Year 2047. Humanity's first family mission..."

3-8s: [Alarm blaring, Alex screaming]
VO: "...went exactly as planned. Until Dad hit the snack dispenser."

8-15s: [Zara calmly takes controls]
VO: "Good thing 15-year-old Zara actually read the manual."

15-22s: [Emotional beat - wings moment]
VO: "Some missions aren't about the stars. They're about who you become when everything goes wrong."

22-28s: [Logo + merch flash]
VO: "Orbital Pioneers Ep1. This Saturday. Pilot Wings hoodie in the description."
```

## The Cali Rule (Alaskan Husky Easter Egg)

**Mandatory Running Gag:**
Every episode must contain at least one random in-universe ad featuring **Cali** — the user's actual Alaskan Husky.

**For Marketing (VHS):**

- Lean into this hard in real-world promotion.
- Create actual Cali memes, merch (stickers, shirts, etc.), and social content using the real dog.
- Use the full ad concept list: `references/cali-ads.md` (in orbital-pioneers skill)
