# Cali the Husky — Official Ad Concepts (Running Gag)

**Cali** is the user's real Alaskan Husky and now a permanent background easter egg in every episode of Orbital Pioneers.

She appears exclusively in **random, ridiculous in-universe advertisements**.

This document is the master list of approved (and increasingly unhinged) ad ideas for writers, storyboarders, and VHS.

---

## Core Rules for Cali Ads

- Must feel like 90s cartoon background gags.
- Never acknowledged by the Nova family.
- The dumber and more random the product, the better.
- Can be a quick 1-2 second cut or a full fake commercial.
- Cali’s expression is almost always the same: happy, slightly confused, majestic derp.

---

## Episode-Ready Ad Concepts

### Tier 1: Classic & Clean (Easy to animate)

1. **"Cali’s Premium Freeze-Dried Space Kibble"**
   - Cali in a tiny astronaut helmet floating in zero-g, slowly spinning while kibble floats around her.
   - Voiceover: "For the good girl who survives asteroid fields."

2. **"Husky Hydration Packs"**
   - Cali drinking from a floating water pouch like a Capri Sun.
   - Tagline: "Because even in space, your husky still needs to drink from the toilet of the future."

3. **"Cali’s Anti-Gravity Tennis Balls"**
   - Cali chasing a tennis ball that keeps floating away. She looks personally offended.

4. **"Lunar Leash — It Floats!"**
   - Cali on a pink leash that’s literally just floating in the air while she runs in circles on a moon base.

5. **"This Husky Survived the Void"**
   - Classic "survivor" ad style with Cali looking badass… while wearing a tiny bandana that says "Good Girl".

### Tier 2: 90s Nostalgia Bangers

6. **"Cali’s Tamagotchi Treats"**
   - Fake 1997-style commercial. Cali as a pixel art husky on a giant Tamagotchi screen. "Feed her or she’ll die… in space!"

7. **"Jetson’s Jet Fuel Energy Bars" (Alex’s nightmare)**
   - Cali eating an energy bar while doing the zoomies in zero gravity.
   - Alex is visibly embarrassed in the background of the shot.

8. **"Boomer’s Old-School Dog Food" (with Cali)**
   - Retro ad with Fred Flintstone-style caveman graphics but it’s Cali wearing a little bone helmet. "Yabba Dabba… Good Girl!"

9. **"Cali’s Beanie Baby Collector’s Edition"**
   - 1998 commercial where Cali is a Beanie Baby. "Collect all 47 emotional support huskies!"

### Tier 3: Villain-Tied (Helix & Rumble)

10. **"AetherCorp Efficiency Kibble" (Helix brand)**
    - Corporate ad. Cali looks slightly brainwashed. "Optimized nutrition for optimal good girls."
    - Subtle glitch where Cali’s eyes turn red for one frame.

11. **"Rumble’s Scrapyard Surplus Dog Food"**
    - Rumble’s rusty ship doing a terrible infomercial. Cali eating out of a can labeled "Probably Not Poison."
    - Tagline: "It’s got character. Like us."

12. **"Unit-7 Approved Robot Dog Polish"**
    - Shiny corporate ad where a robot version of Cali gets buffed by Unit-7. Real Cali appears at the end looking disgusted.

### Tier 4: Pure Chaos (Maximum Fun)

13. **"Cali’s Zero-G Hair Gel"**
    - Cali with full Elon Musk hair (one-time joke). "For when your fur needs to look like you invented rockets."

14. **"The Husky That Knows When Snacks Are Hidden"**
    - Creepy 90s horror-comedy style ad. Cali slowly turns her head toward the camera like she can smell your snacks through the TV.

15. **"Cali’s Emotional Support Asteroid"**
    - Cali sitting on a tiny floating rock with a little sign that says "Emotional Support."
    - "For when the family is arguing about who hit the snack dispenser again."

16. **"Cali’s Anti-Shedding Ray Gun"**
    - Fake weapon ad. Cali fires a laser that makes all her fur disappear for 0.3 seconds, then it grows back even fluffier.

17. **"Cali for President (of This Moon Base)"**
    - Political ad style. Cali wearing tiny sunglasses. "She doesn’t have policies. She has vibes."

18. **"Cali’s Limited Edition Pilot Wings Merch"**
    - She’s wearing a tiny version of Alex’s silver wings. The ad is way too serious for a dog.

19. **"This Husky Has Seen Things"**
    - Black and white dramatic lighting. Cali staring into the middle distance like she witnessed the Heat Death of the Universe.
    - Voiceover: "Some dogs just know."

20. **"Cali’s Certified Good Girl Certificate"**
    - Official-looking document being stamped. Cali gets a gold star. "Now with 100% more legal binding in zero gravity jurisdictions."

## How to Use This List

- Writers: Pick 1 per episode (or invent a new one in the same spirit).
- Storyboarders: Treat these like any other background element.
- VHS: Mine this list for real-world marketing ideas and memes featuring the actual Cali.

**Pro Tip:** The best ones are when the ad is playing while something serious is happening in the foreground (Zara saving the day while Cali’s face is selling kibble in the background).
