## 90s Cartoon Style Rules (for all image gen and animation)

- Bold black outlines
- Flat vibrant colors (deep blacks, oranges, teals, magentas)
- Dramatic lighting and silhouettes
- Gritty but hopeful texture (like 90s animation cels with modern digital coloring)
- Technology feels advanced yet analog (lots of buttons and physical interfaces)
- No modern glitch or hyper-clean effects
