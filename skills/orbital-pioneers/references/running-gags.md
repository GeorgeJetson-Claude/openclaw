# Orbital Pioneers — Running Gags & Easter Eggs

**Rule:** Every episode must include at least one appearance of **Cali** (the user's real Alaskan Husky) as an easter egg.

## Cali the Alaskan Husky

**Description:**  
Cali is the user's actual dog — a beautiful, slightly chaotic Alaskan Husky. She has bright blue eyes and that classic "I’m just happy to be included" derpy expression.

She has been officially added to the show as a running gag.

**Core Rule (Non-Negotiable):**

- Cali must appear in **at least one random advertisement** in every single episode.
- She is never a main character or plot point.
- She is purely a background easter egg / running gag.
- The more random and out-of-place the ad, the better.

### Approved Ways Cali Appears

**In-Universe Ads (Preferred):**

- Billboard on a space station: "Cali’s Premium Freeze-Dried Space Kibble — For Good Girls Who Survive Asteroid Fields"
- TV commercial playing in the background on the Wanderer: Cali in a tiny astronaut helmet doing the "head tilt" while a voiceover says "Cali knows when the snacks are hidden."
- Product placement: A vending machine on a moon base has Cali’s face on it selling "Husky Hydration Packs."
- Magazine ad: Cali wearing sunglasses next to the text "This Husky Doesn’t Need Gravity to Look Cool."
- Sponsored segment: A fake 90s-style commercial break where Cali is the mascot for "Nova Family Fuel" (Alex is embarrassed by it).

**Visual Styles (Mix it up):**

- Photorealistic husky in a retro 90s spacesuit
- Cartoon version of Cali (in the style of the show)
- Robot version of Cali (G.I. Joe style with glowing blue eyes)
- Old-school pixel art version on a screen
- Cali with wild "Elon Musk hair" in one very cursed ad (one-time only joke)

### Tone Guidelines

- Should feel like the random background gags in 90s cartoons (Animaniacs, Rocko’s Modern Life, early Simpsons).
- Can be blink-and-you-miss-it or get a quick 2-second focus.
- Never explained. Never referenced by the main characters.
- The funnier and more random the product Cali is advertising, the better.
