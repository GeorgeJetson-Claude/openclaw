## Chapter 3: The Multi-AI Team System

This was the biggest unlock.

Instead of one person doing everything, we built a persistent team of specialized AI agents that work together:

- **Master Device** (central registry of everyone on the team)
- **Team Room** (shared Markdown + web UI where all agents post updates, hand off tasks, and coordinate)
- **Lobster Workflows** (automated processes that trigger the right agent at the right time)

Example flow for an episode:

1. Human gives high-level direction
2. Grok creates the production plan
3. Claude does script review ("ClaudePass")
4. Gemini generates audio/SFX
5. VHS creates Shorts, thumbnails, and marketing
6. Ledger signs up new affiliates to monetize it
7. Everything gets posted to the Team Room automatically

This system lets us ship extremely fast while keeping quality high.

## Chapter 4: The Cali Strategy (Turning a Running Gag Into Money)

We added a silly rule: Every episode must contain at least one random ad featuring the user's actual dog, **Cali** (an Alaskan Husky).

This started as a fun 90s-style easter egg.

Then we realized: **This is a revenue engine**.

- Cali appears in ads inside the show → fans love it
- We turn those ads into real merch (stickers, shirts, digital packs)
- We sell "Cali Ad Prompt Packs" and storybooks on Gumroad + Etsy
- The real dog becomes the mascot

Lesson: Turn your dumbest running gags into products. People will buy them.

## Chapter 5: The Villain System (Batman + 90s Fun)

We stole the best idea from Batman: multiple villains per episode.

- One you can defeat in a fight
- One you have to outsmart
- One that escapes (To Be Continued)

Our two main recurring villains:

- **Director Helix** (corporate G.I. Joe Cobra type with Inspector Gadget gadgets + Elon Musk hair)
- **Captain Rumble** (chaotic space pirate with broken robots + wild hair)

This structure keeps episodes fun and replayable while building long-term story.
