# Cali's Space Adventures

**A Collection of Silly Stories Featuring the Goodest Girl in the Galaxy**

An Orbital Pioneers Story

Featuring Cali the Husky (the official mascot of Orbital Pioneers)

## Story 1: The Floating Kibble Incident

Cali was minding her own business, floating peacefully in zero gravity on the space station, when suddenly...

A bag of "Premium Freeze-Dried Space Kibble" tore open.

Kibble began orbiting her head like tiny asteroids.

Cali did what any good girl would do.

She started eating them mid-air, doing slow-motion barrel rolls while catching pieces with her mouth.

An entire tour group of alien children watched in awe.

One little alien whispered: "That is the most powerful being I have ever seen."

Cali, with kibble stuck to her nose, just did the head tilt.

**Moral:** Sometimes the best strategy is to enjoy the chaos.

## Story 2: The Emotional Support Asteroid

The Nova family was arguing again.

Alex wanted to take the dangerous shortcut.
Maya wanted to follow protocol.
Zara was already halfway through the shortcut.

Leo was stress-eating space snacks.

In the middle of all this, a small asteroid slowly floated past the window.

Sitting on top of it, perfectly calm, was Cali.

She had somehow acquired a tiny "Emotional Support" vest and was just... chilling.

Every time someone raised their voice, she would slowly turn her head and give them The Look.

The argument stopped.

Alex quietly said: "Okay. Let's do it Maya's way."

Cali gave one single approving boop to the window.

**Moral:** Sometimes you don't need to say anything. You just need to exist with judgment.

## Story 3: Director Helix Tries to Optimize Cali

Director Helix's ship docked with the Wanderer.

He stepped out with Unit-7 and a swarm of tiny drones.

"Family unit efficiency is at 67%. We are here to help."

He scanned the ship.

His eyes landed on Cali, who was currently trying to eat her own tail in zero gravity.

Helix's eye twitched.

"This... creature is operating at maximum inefficiency. We will optimize her."

Unit-7 approached Cali with a "Compliance Collar."

Cali looked at the collar.

Then looked at Unit-7.

Then did the slowest, most dramatic head tilt in recorded history.

Unit-7's systems glitched for 0.8 seconds.

Helix's hair became slightly more unkempt.

"New directive," Helix said quietly. "Do not optimize the dog."

**Moral:** Some things are already perfect.

## Story 4: The Great Vending Machine Heist

Captain Rumble and his crew boarded the station looking for parts.

They found the greatest treasure of all:

A fully stocked 90s-style vending machine.

Rumble's eyes lit up.

"Boys... we're rich."

They started loading up on snacks.

That's when Cali appeared.

She didn't bark. She didn't growl.

She just sat down in front of the machine and gave them the most disappointed look a husky has ever given anyone.

Rumble's hand slowly lowered the bag of chips.

"...We were just... borrowing them?"

Cali continued staring.

Rumble sighed the sigh of a defeated space pirate.

"Fine. Put it back."

As they left, Cali gave one single approving tail wag.

**Moral:** Even pirates have standards when a good girl is watching.

## Story 5: The Wings

(This one is a little different)

After the events of the First Leap, when everything had calmed down...

Cali found the silver pilot wings where Alex had dropped them.

She carried them gently in her mouth and placed them at Zara's feet.

Zara looked down at the dog, then at the wings.

Cali did the head tilt.

Zara smiled, picked up the wings, and clipped them onto Cali's little space vest.

Cali looked extremely proud.

From that day forward, whenever anyone on the ship was having a hard time, they would look over and see a very serious Alaskan Husky wearing pilot wings, floating past.

And somehow... things felt a little more okay.

**Moral:** The best heroes sometimes have four legs and don't need to say a single word.
