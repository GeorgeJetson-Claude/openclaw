# Orbital Pioneers Digital Products Revenue Plan (Etsy + Gumroad)

**Goal:** Turn the free work we're already doing into high-margin digital PDFs that sell on Etsy and Gumroad while we ship the cartoon.

## Core Philosophy

- One piece of creative work (the show) should fuel multiple products.
- Digital products = almost 100% margin after creation.
- Use Etsy for discovery + Gumroad for higher-ticket bundles.
- Leverage Cali the Husky heavily (she's our secret weapon).

## Current Products (Already Built)

1. **How We Made a 90s Cartoon in 2026 Using Only Free Tools**
   - Meta playbook (highest perceived value)
   - Strong for creators + AI-curious audience

2. **Cali's Space Adventures**
   - Fun, light, shareable stories
   - Appeals to kids + nostalgic adults
   - Excellent for bundles and merch cross-sell

## Recommended Product Roadmap

**Phase 1 (This Week)**

- Finish & list the two above
- Create "Cali Ad Prompt Pack" (40+ concepts + image prompts)
- Create "Villain Design Guide + Prompt Pack"

**Phase 2 (Next 2 Weeks)**

- "Orbital Pioneers Character Bible" (sellable version of the internal bible)
- "90s Cartoon Prompt Bible" (general, not just our show)
- Short "Cali Activity Book" (coloring pages, puzzles, etc. — parents buy these)

**Phase 3**

- Episode companion eBooks (after each episode drops)
- "How to Build Your Own Multi-AI Team" (system-building product)
- Subscription: "Cali Ad of the Month" digital pack

## Pricing Strategy

- Single eBooks: $4.99 – $9.99
- Prompt packs: $7 – $12
- Bundles: $14.99 – $24.99 (much higher perceived value)
- Use Etsy for lower price points + discovery
- Use Gumroad for bundles + higher ticket items

## Marketing (VHS Handles This)

- Post Cali content constantly (she's the hook)
- "Behind the scenes of making a 90s cartoon" content performs well
- Cross-promote between all 11+ websites
- Use the real dog in photos for authenticity
